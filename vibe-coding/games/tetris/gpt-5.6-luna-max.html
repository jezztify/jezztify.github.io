Keyboard controls

  • Left/Right Move left / right
  • Down Soft drop, no score
  • Up Rotate clockwise
  • Space Hard drop and lock
  • P Pause or resume gameplay
  • R Restart from any state

Use the arrow keys to move, Up to rotate clockwise, and Space to drop.
